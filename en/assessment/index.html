Quick assessment

What your device needs to get Russian-origin status

Six questions, about a minute. We will show a preliminary route under Government Decrees No. 719 and No. 1875 and a list of documents worth preparing. This is a guide, not a score calculation: points and the ad valorem share are set for each specific position of Section VII of Decree 719 and verified by an expert.

1. What kind of product is it?
2. Do you have a Roszdravnadzor registration certificate?
3. Do you know the classification codes? (OKPD2 and the device-type code from the NKMI nomenclature)
4. Where and how is the product made today?
5. Why do you need Russian-origin status?
6. Is the product in the Russian industrial products register (GISP)?

Please answer all questions to see the route.

Preliminary route

    What to prepare for an exact check

    • A description of the device and the registration certificate (if you have one).
    • OKPD2 and NKMI device-type codes.
    • A bill of materials with the origin and cost of components.
    • Production sites, the list of operations and contractor agreements.
    • The tenders you plan to take part in.

    This is a guide based on your answers, not a legal opinion or a score calculation. Applicable requirements depend on the position in Section VII of Decree 719 and on the particular tender. The route steps are practical recommendations, not legal norms.
